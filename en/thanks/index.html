Thank you for buying Easy Corona

It is a single app for every version: you download it and activate it with your key.

  1. Download and install Easy Corona

    If you already have it installed, skip this step.

    Download for Windows 10 and 11 Mac with Apple silicon M1 or later Mac with Intel

    If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway.

    On a Mac (macOS 15 or later; the type of Mac is shown in the Apple menu → About This Mac): open the .dmg and drag EasyCorona to Applications. If macOS says it couldn't verify it when you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  2. Check your email

    In a few minutes you'll receive your license key, at the email address you paid with. If you don't see it within 15 minutes, check your spam folder too or write to zu3run@gmail.com.

  3. Activate your license

    In Easy Corona: License → Activate license… menu, paste the whole key and accept. You'll see your version at the top.
