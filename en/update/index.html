Free update

Easy Corona 1.4.2

It installs over the previous version and keeps your license, your presets and your projects. If you're coming from an older version, below you also have everything released since.

What's new in 1.4.2

  • The preview is always on when you open the app. It no longer stays off when you open a project or a preset saved with it unticked. The quality you choose is remembered for next time and, if you turn it off, the image tells you it is paused.
  • Double-click a slider to put it back to its starting value, as in Lightroom.
  • Faster saving. Ctrl+S (⌘S on Mac) saves the open project without asking; «Save project as…» picks another place. And in the «Project» menu, «Open recent» and «Exit».
  • No closing halfway. If you close the window while it creates the HDR or enhances the corona, it asks first.
  • Cleaner presets to share. They no longer carry the Moon marked by hand on another photo or options that belong to each project.
  • Help → Step-by-step tutorial and About Easy Corona (your version and your license, what you need if you write to support).
  • Fixes: the preview of some projects saved halfway through «Create HDR» was black; photos from two focal lengths with one camera rotated relative to the other now combine properly; the «Simple mode / Advanced mode» header marks the one of the page you are on; a hidden «Process log» stays hidden.

What's new in 1.4.1

  • «Remove the stars» finds more. It now also removes misshapen stars (the ones that moved during stacking and show up as several dots together) and faint colored stars, which used to stay. «As is» and «Clean» don't change.

What's new in 1.4

  • Remove the stars (beta). For a clean sky: the app removes almost all the stars (the faint and misshapen ones too) and the hot pixels, without putting any back. Whatever can't be told apart from the corona stays. In Simple mode it's the «Remove the stars» checkbox; in «Final result», the «Remove them» mode (next to «As is» and «Clean»).
  • Better fill where the stars were. It follows the edges of the corona and copies only the grain of the nearby sky: no more bands, notches or leftovers of the dark ring around bright stars. With «Clean» too, and faster (the preview, in a second and a half).

What's new in 1.3

  • Clean stars (beta). The app finds the stars in the HDR, removes the dark ring the filters leave around them and puts them back with the brightness and the number you choose; it also removes hot pixels. In Simple mode («Stars» and «How many») and in «Final result», with their mask for other programs.
  • Now in English too. The app opens in the language of your computer and can be changed in View → Idioma · Language. The website, the license agreement and the installer are in English too.
  • Step-by-step tutorial on the website: every feature of the app, with screenshots.

What's new in 1.2

  • Chromosphere and prominences. The fastest photos are stacked separately, without the Moon, and added to the result: sharp prominences even if the Moon moved during totality. If they are burned out in every photo, the app recovers their color.
  • A single, sharp Moon. The HDR no longer leaves “ghosts” at the edge of the Moon from stacking photos with the Moon in different places.
  • Earthshine. Brings out the lunar maria with the long photos, or straight from the HDR if they show in it.
  • Photos with two focal lengths (beta). Combines the photos from two lenses, for example 600 and 300 mm: the detail of the long focal length with the field of the short one, with no visible seam.
  • Faster. Aligning, 4 to 5 times faster; the enhancement passes, up to 37 times. If you only change the final result, «Run the process» redoes only that step.
  • A clearer final result. The sky, the Moon and the chromosphere, each in its own box, and «Inner corona» to soften the enhancement next to the Moon.
  • Practice photos. If you don't have your photos yet, practice with zu2pic's (Home → Practice photos).
  • TIFF for other programs. The 16-bit TIFF looks the same in Photoshop, Lightroom and Affinity. And the text can be made bigger in View → Text size.
  • Fixes: the sky gradient is subtracted in linear and no longer distorts the bright corona; the fine detail adjusts itself to each HDR; the final brightness no longer switches to manual when opening a project.

  1. Download version 1.4.2

    Download for Windows 10 and 11 Mac with Apple silicon M1 or later Mac with Intel

  2. Install it over the old one

    Close Easy Corona before installing.

    Windows: open the installer and follow the steps; it replaces the previous version. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway.

    Mac (macOS 15 or later): open the .dmg, drag EasyCorona to Applications and choose Replace. If macOS says it couldn't verify it when you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. Open it

    At the top you'll see “Easy Corona 1.4.2” and your version. If the free version showed up, go to License → Activate license… and paste again the key you received by email. Any problem: zu3run@gmail.com.
